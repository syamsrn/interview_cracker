## Guardrails

- Keep the app local-first and privacy-focused.
- Prefer free local components where practical.
- Do not introduce paid services as requirements.
- Do not build stealth, proctoring-bypass, or hidden-assistance behavior.
- Keep each stage small, tested, and documented before moving forward.
